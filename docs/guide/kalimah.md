# Al-Kalimah: Parts of Speech (أقسام الكلمة)

Every meaningful single word (**كَلِمَة**) in Arabic belongs to one of three exhaustive categories:

1. **Al-Ism (الِاسْم — Noun)**: Indicates a meaning in itself without being bound to tense (past, present, or future).
2. **Al-Fi'l (الفِعْل — Verb)**: Indicates an event or action bound to one of the three tenses (*Madi*, *Mudari'*, or *Amr*).
3. **Al-Harf (الحَرْف — Particle)**: Indicates a meaning only when joined to another word (*harf ja'a li-ma'nan*).

## Diagnostic Signs of *Al-Ism* (علامات الاسم)

Ibn Ajurrum states that the noun is recognized by four principal signs:

| Sign | Arabic | Example | Explanation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Genitive Case (*Khafd / Jarr*)** | الخَفْض / الجَرّ | بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ | Taking a genitive marker via preposition, construct state (*idafah*), or apposition (*taba'iyyah*). |
| **Nunation (*Tanwin*)** | التَّنْوِين | كِتَابٌ / كِتَابًا / كِتَابٍ | An unvoweled *nun* pronounced at the end of a noun but not written. |
| **Definite Article (*Al-*)** | دُخُولُ الأَلِفِ وَاللَّام | القَلَمُ، العِلْمُ | Prefixing `ال` of definition. |
| **Prepositions (*Huruf al-Khafd*)** | حُرُوفُ الخَفْض | مِنْ، إِلَى، عَنْ، عَلَى، فِي، رُبَّ، البَاء، الكَاف، اللَّام | Entering after any of the particles of *jarr* or oath (*huruf al-qasam*). |

## Diagnostic Signs of *Al-Fi'l* (علامات الفعل)

- **قَدْ (*Qad*)**: Enters upon both the past (*qad aflaha*) and present (*qad ya'lamu*) verb.
- **السِّين / سَوْفَ (*Sin* & *Sawfa*)**: Exclusive to the imperfect verb (*Al-Fi'l Al-Mudari'*), indicating near or distant future.
- **تَاءُ التَّأْنِيثِ السَّاكِنَة (*Ta' al-Ta'nith al-Sakinah*)**: Exclusive to the past verb (*Al-Fi'l Al-Madi*), e.g., قَالَتْ.

## Diagnostic Sign of *Al-Harf* (علامة الحرف)

A particle is defined negatively: **مَا لَا يَصْلُحُ مَعَهُ دَلِيلُ الِاسْمِ وَلَا دَلِيلُ الفِعْلِ** — that which accepts neither any sign of the noun nor any sign of the verb.
