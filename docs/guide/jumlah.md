# Al-Jumlah: Sentence Structure (الجملة العربية)

Complete Arabic sentences (*Al-Jumlah Al-Mufidah*) divide into two foundational structures depending on the word that initiates the predication (*isnad*):

## 1. Al-Jumlah Al-Ismiyyah (الجملة الاسمية — Nominal Sentence)

A nominal sentence begins with a noun and consists of two essential pillars (*ruknan*):

- **Al-Mubtada' (المُبْتَدَأ — Subject)**: A nominative (*marfu'*) noun stripped of explicit verbal governing agents (*'awamil lafziyyah*), typically definite (*ma'rifah*).
- **Al-Khabar (الخَبَر — Predicate)**: The nominative (*marfu'*) part that completes the benefit alongside the *Mubtada'*.

### Types of *Khabar* (أنواع الخبر)

| Type | Example | Analysis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mufrad (Single Word)** | العِلْمُ نُورٌ | `نُورٌ` is *khabar marfu'* with explicit *dammah*. |
| **Jumlah Ismiyyah** | الحَدِيقَةُ أَشْجَارُهَا مُثْمِرَةٌ | The inner clause `أَشْجَارُهَا مُثْمِرَةٌ` is in the place of *raf'* (*fi mahalli raf'*) as *khabar*. |
| **Jumlah Fi'liyyah** | الطَّالِبُ يَجْتَهِدُ | The verbal clause `يَجْتَهِدُ` is in the place of *raf'* as *khabar*. |
| **Shibh Jumlah (Prepositional / Adverbial)** | الكِتَابُ عَلَى المَكْتَبِ | The prepositional phrase relates to an elided predicate (*khabar mahdhuf*). |

## 2. Al-Jumlah Al-Fi'liyyah (الجملة الفعلية — Verbal Sentence)

A verbal sentence begins with a verb (*fi'l*) and requires a doer (*fa'il*) or deputy doer (*na'ib al-fa'il*):

- **Al-Fi'l + Al-Fa'il (الفعل والفاعل)**: When the verb is active (*mabni lil-ma'lum*), its agent (*fa'il*) is always nominative (*marfu'*), e.g., جَاءَ الحَقُّ.
- **Al-Fi'l + Na'ib Al-Fa'il (الفعل ونائب الفاعل)**: When the verb is passive (*mabni lil-majhul*), the direct object takes the nominative position of the agent, e.g., كُتِبَ الدَّرْسُ.
