# Al-I'rab & Al-Bina' (باب الإعراب والبناء)

**Al-I'rab (الإِعْرَاب)** is the change of the endings of words due to the difference in governing agents (*'awamil*) entering upon them, either explicitly (*lafzan*) or implicitly (*taqdiran*).

**Al-Bina' (البِنَاء)** is the fixedness of a word's ending in a single state regardless of governing agents.

## The Four States of *I'rab* (أقسام الإعراب الأربعة)

| State | Arabic | Primary Marker | Applies To |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Nominative (*Raf'*)** | الرَّفْع | الضَّمَّة (*Dammah*) | *Ism* & *Fi'l Mudari'* |
| **2. Accusative (*Nasb*)** | النَّصْب | الفَتْحَة (*Fathah*) | *Ism* & *Fi'l Mudari'* |
| **3. Genitive (*Khafd / Jarr*)** | الخَفْض / الجَرّ | الكَسْرَة (*Kasrah*) | *Ism* exclusively |
| **4. Jussive (*Jazm*)** | الجَزْم | السُّكُون (*Sukun*) | *Fi'l Mudari'* exclusively |

## Summary of Declension Markers by Word Class

| Word Class | Raf' | Nasb | Jarr |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Singular Noun (*Al-Ism Al-Mufrad*)** | Dammah (`ـُ`) | Fathah (`ـَ`) | Kasrah (`ـِ`) |
| **Broken Plural (*Jam' al-Taksir*)** | Dammah (`ـُ`) | Fathah (`ـَ`) | Kasrah (`ـِ`) |
| **Sound Feminine Plural (*Jam' al-Mu'annath al-Salim*)** | Dammah (`ـُ`) | Kasrah (`ـِ`) | Kasrah (`ـِ`) |
| **Dual (*Al-Muthanna*)** | Alif (`ـَانِ`) | Ya' (`ـَيْنِ`) | Ya' (`ـَيْنِ`) |
| **Sound Masculine Plural (*Jam' al-Mudhakkar al-Salim*)** | Waw (`ـُونَ`) | Ya' (`ـِينَ`) | Ya' (`ـِينَ`) |
| **The Five Nouns (*Al-Asma' Al-Khamsah*)** | Waw (`أَبُوكَ`) | Alif (`أَبَاكَ`) | Ya' (`أَبِيكَ`) |
| **Diptote (*Al-Mamnu' min al-Sarf*)** | Dammah (`ـُ`) | Fathah (`ـَ`) | Fathah (`ـَ`) |
