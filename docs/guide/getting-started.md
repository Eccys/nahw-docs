# Getting Started (المقدمة في علم النحو)

**An-Nahw (النَّحْو)** is the classical science of Arabic syntax that governs the endings of words (*awakhir al-kalim*) according to their grammatical roles in a sentence, distinguishing declinable (*mu'rab*) from indeclinable (*mabni*) words.

## Definition of Speech (*Al-Kalam*)

In the tradition of *Al-Muqaddimah Al-Ajurrumiyyah* (المقدمة الآجرومية), grammatical speech (**الكَلَام**) requires four simultaneous conditions:

| Condition | Arabic Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Utterance** | اللَّفْظ (*Al-Lafz*) | A voiced sound composed of Arabic alphabetic letters (*huruf hijaiyyah*). |
| **2. Compound** | المُرَكَّب (*Al-Murakkab*) | Composed of two or more words (explicitly or implicitly). |
| **3. Complete** | المُفِيد (*Al-Mufid*) | Conveys a complete benefit such that the listener is not left waiting for the core predicate. |
| **4. Intentional / Conventional** | بِالوَضْع (*Bil-Wad'*) | Spoken with intent according to the established conventions of the Arabic language. |

## Core Curriculum Path

1. **[Al-Kalimah (Parts of Speech)](/guide/kalimah)** — Distinguishing *Ism* (noun), *Fi'l* (verb), and *Harf* (particle) through their morphological and syntactic signs (*'alamat*).
2. **[Al-I'rab & Al-Bina'](/guide/irab)** — Mastering the four grammatical states (*Raf'*, *Nasb*, *Jarr*, *Jazm*) and their primary and secondary markers.
3. **[Al-Jumlah (Sentence Structure)](/guide/jumlah)** — Analyzing *Al-Jumlah Al-Ismiyyah* (*Mubtada'* and *Khabar*) and *Al-Jumlah Al-Fi'liyyah* (*Fi'l* and *Fa'il*).
